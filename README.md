Green Hat 🔰
A free, beginner-friendly PWA for new CNC lathe operators — hobbyists, students, and anyone just getting started on the machine.
The problem it solves
New operators get put on a lathe with minimal instruction. Touch-off is confusing, the terminology is unfamiliar, and one wrong move means a crash, a scrapped part, or worse. There's no shortage of advanced machinist tools and calculators online — but almost nothing designed for the person who doesn't yet know what X diameter mode means or why stickout matters.
What Green Hat does
It walks a beginner through the things an experienced operator does automatically — setting up the machine, touching off tools correctly, understanding what the control is telling them, and leaving clear notes for the next person. It's a guided reference and note-taking tool, not a calculator or G-code generator.
It now includes setup status, last setup/run fields, attention flags, timestamped note logs, exportable handoff summaries, beginner definitions, and expanded symbols/meanings for common lathe and control terms.
The touch-off flow includes a G7/G8 diameter-vs-radius warning, but treats that as control-dependent. Beginners are told to check the program safety block, setup sheet, active modal display, and machine manual instead of assuming one code means the same thing on every lathe.
Who it's for
The hobbyist with a lathe in the garage who learned from YouTube
The new hire on the shop floor who hasn't had formal training
The student in a trade program who needs a quick reference at the machine
Anyone who still second-guesses themselves on touch-off
What it's not
It's not a G-code tool. It's not a production planner. It's not for experienced machinists. Those tools already exist. Green Hat is for the person who just needs to get through their first setup without breaking anything.
