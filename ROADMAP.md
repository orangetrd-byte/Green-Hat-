# Green Hat Roadmap

## Phase 1: Beginner Safety Baseline

- Keep touch-off, setup notes, pre-flight checks, and beginner definitions clear.
- Preserve mobile-first offline PWA behavior.
- Maintain build information, version number, and MGP identity.
- Keep the app focused on new CNC lathe operators.

## Phase 2: Guided Setup Flow

- Improve step-by-step touch-off guidance.
- Add clearer handoff and print-friendly setup outputs.
- Strengthen shop-specific rules and checklist customization.

## Phase 3: Beginner Reference Quality

- Expand simple definitions, diagrams, and safe verification prompts.
- Improve speeds and feeds references only at beginner level.
- Avoid advanced calculator, simulator, or G-code generator scope.

## Phase 4: Classroom and Shop Polish

- Improve readability on phones and shop tablets.
- Add better empty states and saved setup recovery.
- Keep language plain, calm, and beginner-safe.
